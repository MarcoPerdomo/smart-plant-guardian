# Clarify Sentia’s public journey

## Homepage
- Move the complete five-step **How it works today** story from About to the homepage.
- Place it directly after the opening showcase so new visitors see the intended journey early:
  1. Add your plants
  2. Keep a diary for each plant
  3. Use Sentia’s care tools when needed
  4. Engage with the community
  5. Trade with trusted plant owners, marked **Coming soon**
- Keep the existing visual language, icons, spacing, colours, and mobile behaviour.

## About page
- Remove **How it works today** so the journey is explained in only one place.
- Keep **Why Sentia?**, but make its three themes:
  - **Connect with your plants**, understand their needs and record their health, care, and progress in a journal.
  - **AI care guidance**, retain the current description.
  - **A plant-loving community**, retain the current description.
- Remove the marketplace theme from this section.

## Get Started guide
- Rework the page into seven practical steps:
  1. **Create your account**, retain the current guidance.
  2. **Add your first plant**, retain the current guidance.
  3. **Journal your plants**, record watering, fertilising, pruning, repotting, flowering, photos, and other care.
  4. **Use Sentia’s tools when you need help**, including weather guidance, AI care, and camera-based disease checks.
  5. **Engage with the community**, share progress, let journal activity appear socially, comment, add friends, and chat.
  6. **Help the community**, ask for help with photos and contribute useful care knowledge so plants are understood as living beings.
  7. **Marketplace**, marked **Coming soon**, explaining trusted buying, selling, and swapping.
- Remove the old dedicated sensor, weather, and Raspberry Pi camera steps.
- Update the page introduction and search/social description so they match the new practical guide and no longer lead with sensors.

## Technical details
- Reuse the existing page patterns and shared visual tokens; this is a content and layout change only.
- Keep all current links and account actions working.
- Verify all three pages on desktop and mobile, including step ordering, the Coming soon labels, and metadata.
