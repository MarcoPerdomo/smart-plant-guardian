# Smart Plant Guardian

Help me create an application and a website where I plug in some Arduinos, and read data from different sensors. I can keep up with the progress of my interior plants through sensors like damp, moisture, radar sensors for pest controls, cameras for a live streaming of the current state of my plant. Additionally, I wanna keep track of how my plants are doing over time, through built tables in the backend. With the data I collect, I also wanna know what is the best time to water them, give them more sun, controls pests, potential diseases, potential issues with leaves, etc. I wanna be able to plug it any plant, so I would need to have a compilation of thousands of house plants with their ideal conditions, and care instructions. I would like to have a dashboard to see each plant, with their nickname, overall status, predicted watering and a weekly or 2-3 times per week notification on the status on my plant, based on a short AI summary.
